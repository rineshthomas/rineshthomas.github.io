How we use information

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and improve our websites, apps, software, and services.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us host websites, process email, provide cloud infrastructure, process payments, maintain records, or deliver support. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and security, or as part of a business transfer with appropriate notice.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards for personal information. Personal and sensitive data should be transmitted using HTTPS or other modern cryptography where applicable. No internet or email system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including support, service delivery, legal, tax, accounting, security, and dispute-resolution requirements. Support requests are typically retained for up to 24 months unless a longer period is required. Business, accounting, and contract records may be retained for longer where required by law.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by emailing support@logicleaptechnologies.com. If a Logicleap app allows account creation, the account and associated app data can be requested for deletion using the instructions on the Data Deletion page. We may need to verify your request and may retain certain records where required for security, fraud prevention, legal, tax, or accounting reasons.

Children

Our website and business services are not directed to children. If a specific app is intended for children or families, it will provide additional disclosures and comply with applicable platform and privacy requirements.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
