Support

Product and app support.

For support with a Logicleap product, app, invoice, or service engagement, email support with the relevant product name and a clear description of the issue.

Contact support

Email: support@logicleaptechnologies.com

For paid apps, in-app purchases, or service-related payment questions, we aim to respond within three business days. If Google Play or another platform contacts us about an urgent product issue, we aim to respond within 24 hours.

What to include

  • Product, app, or project name.
  • Device, operating system, browser, or platform where the issue occurs.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, screenshots, and any order or invoice reference if applicable.
  • The email address used for the product or purchase, if relevant.

Privacy and data requests

For access, correction, or deletion requests, use the instructions on the Data Deletion page or email support@logicleaptechnologies.com.
