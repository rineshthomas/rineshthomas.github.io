A senior engineering model for small, serious scopes.

The work is shaped around clear output: a feature, a fix, a tool, a migration, a review, or a system that can be handed over without guesswork.

Trace

Understand the actual system

Build

Make the smallest durable change that solves the real problem and fits the surrounding codebase.
